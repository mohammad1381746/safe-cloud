What happens next
  1. The file is hashed (SHA-256/MD5) and staged on the Scanner Server as it uploads.
  2. It's checked for encryption/password-protection before any scanner touches it - the configured encrypted-file policy applies (see Settings).
  3. Every scanner in the selected profile runs in its own isolated, network-disabled container; results are combined per the profile's aggregation policy.
  4. You're redirected to the scan's detail page immediately - refresh it to watch the status move from RECEIVED to a final verdict.
